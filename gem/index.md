# Gilt Edged Models (GEM)

One reason agent-based models are useful for analysing relationships in complex systems is because they can consume (import) real world inputs. Agent-based model *liquidity preference \- extended* (ABMLP-X) is a computational (agent-based) monetary system model. The model is a work in progress. Visit [giltedged.systems](https://www.giltedged.systems) for more about ABMLP-X and earlier models.

### Setting the Scene

Imagine in our vast and expanding multiverse there is another world remarkably similar to Earth. In this other world there exists a nation state, a peculiar place with an equally peculiar name; ABMLP-X. Remarkably, ABMLP-X is synchronous \- chronologically, culturally and institutionally \- with planet Earth’s the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (UK). ABMLP-X could be described as a scaled down parallel system; not a duplicate, but eerily familiar to the UK. Read on about a dynamic [system state](/gem/abmlp-x-dynamic-system-state.md).
