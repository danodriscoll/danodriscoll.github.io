Setting the Scene

Imagine in our vast and expanding multiverse there is another world remarkably similar to Earth. In this other world there exists a nation state, a peculiar place with an equally peculiar name; ABMLP-X. Remarkably, ABMLP-X is synchronous - chronologically, culturally and institutionally - with planet Earth's the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (UK). ABMLP-X could be described as a scaled down parallel system; not a duplicate, but eerily familiar to the UK.
